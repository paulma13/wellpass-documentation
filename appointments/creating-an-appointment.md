# How to create an appointment

## Overview

You can book a client into an appointment slot directly from the appointments board. The flow walks you through selecting a service, finding or creating the client, optionally applying a package, and confirming the booking.

---

## How to create an appointment

### Step 1 — Open the booking form

1. Go to **Calendar** in the left sidebar and switch to the **Appointments** view.
2. Click an available time slot in the staff member's column for the time you want to book.
3. The **Create Appointment** modal opens.

### Step 2 — Select a service

1. Choose the appointment service from the service selector.
2. The **Price** field is pre-filled with the service's default price. You can change it if needed — see the price override section below.
3. Add any **Notes** for this appointment if needed.

### Step 3 — Find or add the client

Use the search field to find the client you want to book.

- Type the client's name. Results appear as you type.
- Select the client from the list to confirm.

If the client does not appear in the search results, you can add them as a walk-in. You will be asked to enter their first name, last name, and phone number. Phone number is required for walk-in clients.

> **Note:** Always search before adding a new client. This prevents duplicate records when the same person returns in the future.

### Step 4 — Select a package (if applicable)

After the client is selected, a **Use a Package?** step appears if the client has eligible packages.

- All eligible packages are listed. The first one is pre-selected by default.
- You can switch to a different package by selecting it from the list.
- To book without using a package, deselect all packages. The booking is then recorded as a cash booking.

If the client has no eligible packages, this step is skipped.

### Step 5 — Confirm the booking

Click **Create** (or the confirm button) to finalise the appointment. The appointment card appears immediately in the staff member's column on the board.

---

## Settings and options

### Price override

The price field is pre-filled from the service's default price. You can edit it before confirming.

If you change the price, a **Reason for price change...** field appears and is required. Enter a reason before you can save the appointment.

### Notes

The notes field is optional and is visible to staff from the appointment detail.

### Walk-in clients

Walk-in clients are added with their name and phone number only — they do not need a Wellpass account. Phone number is required.

Walk-in clients do not receive push notifications as they have no app account. The package selection step is skipped for walk-in clients who have not previously visited your studio.

### Package use

When a package is applied, the booking uses one session from that package. When no package is applied, the booking is recorded as a cash booking at the listed price.

Only active packages with sessions remaining that are valid for appointments appear in the package selection step.
