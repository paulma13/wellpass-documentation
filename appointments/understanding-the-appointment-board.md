# Understanding the appointment board

## What the appointment board is

The appointments board is the daily view on the **Calendar** page. It shows all staff members who have the Appointments role enabled, arranged side by side as columns. Each row represents a time slot, and the entire view covers the full working day from top to bottom.

The board gives you a live overview of every staff member's schedule for the selected date — who is booked, who is available, and when time has been blocked off.

---

## Board layout

Each staff member with the Appointments role enabled has their own column. Their name appears at the top of the column. The time axis runs vertically down the page.

You can navigate to different dates using the date controls at the top of the page. The board always shows one day at a time.

---

## Colour states

Each time slot on the board has a visual state that tells you at a glance what is happening:

| State | Appearance | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Available** | White / empty | The staff member is within their working hours and has no booking or block. The slot can be booked. |
| **Blocked** | Grey striped | The staff member has a manual availability block for this time. The slot cannot be booked. |
| **Outside schedule** | Dark grey | The time falls outside the staff member's regular working hours. The slot is not bookable. |
| **Booked** | Magenta card | A client is booked into this slot. The card shows the client name and service. |

---

## Reading a booked appointment card

Each magenta appointment card on the board shows:
- The client's name
- The appointment service

Click the card to open the appointment detail modal, where you can view full details, edit the appointment, update attendance, or cancel it.

---

## Clicking an available slot

Clicking any white (available) slot opens the **Create Appointment** modal pre-filled with the staff member and time you clicked. This is the quickest way to create a new booking directly from the board.

---

## Staff who don't appear

A staff member only appears as a column on the board if they have the **Appointments** checkbox enabled in their profile under the Role section. Staff without this role are not shown on the board, even if they have services assigned.
