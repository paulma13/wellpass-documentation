# How to view the appointments board

## Overview

The appointments board is on the **Calendar** page and shows all staff members' schedules side by side for a given day. Each column represents one staff member, and each booked appointment appears as a card in that column. Open slots and blocked times are also visible so you can see availability at a glance.

---

## Switching to the appointments board

1. Open **Calendar** from the left sidebar.
2. If your studio offers both classes and appointments, a **Select** dropdown appears at the top of the page. Click it and choose **Appointments**.
3. If your studio offers appointments only, the appointments board loads automatically.

---

## Navigating by date

Use the date controls at the top of the page to move between days:

- **Left and right arrows** — move one day backward or forward.
- **Today** button — jumps back to the current date.
- **Date label** — click it to open a date picker popover and jump to any date directly.

---

## Searching for a specific appointment

A **Search Appointments by client name...** field appears in the top-right area of the page header. Type a client's name to filter the board and find their appointment quickly.
