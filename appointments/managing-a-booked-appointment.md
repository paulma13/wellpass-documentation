# How to manage a booked appointment

## Overview

After an appointment is booked, you can update the assigned staff member, adjust the price, edit notes, reschedule the appointment to a different time, or cancel it. All of these actions are available from the appointment detail modal on the board.

---

## How to open an appointment

1. Go to **Calendar** in the left sidebar and switch to the **Appointments** view.
2. Find the appointment card on the board and click it.
3. The **appointment detail modal** opens.

---

## Editing appointment details

From the appointment detail modal, the following fields are editable:

### Staff member

You can reassign the appointment to a different staff member. If the new staff member does not offer the selected service, a warning is shown. You can still save the reassignment if needed.

### Price

The price can be changed after booking. If you update the price, a **Reason for price change...** field appears and is required. Enter a reason before saving.

### Notes

The notes field can be updated at any time. Notes are visible to staff from the appointment detail.

---

## Rescheduling an appointment

To move an appointment to a different time or staff member, drag the appointment card to the new time slot on the board, or open the appointment detail and update the time from there.

---

## Cancelling an appointment

To cancel an appointment:

1. Open the appointment detail modal.
2. Click the **Cancel** button. A confirmation dialog appears.
3. Review the cancellation details:
   - If the client paid by card online, a **Refund online card payment** toggle appears. Turn it on to issue a refund through the payment system.
   - If the client booked using a package, their session is automatically returned to their package. No action is needed.
4. Confirm the cancellation.

The appointment is removed from the board and the client is notified.

### Refund online card payment

When the cancelled appointment was paid by card online, the cancellation dialog shows a **Refund online card payment** toggle.

- **Toggle off (default):** The appointment is cancelled but no card refund is issued. Use this if the client has already been refunded by other means or if no refund is appropriate.
- **Toggle on:** The system processes a refund of the card payment before cancelling.

### Package session return

If the appointment was booked using a package, the session is automatically returned to the client's package when the appointment is cancelled. This happens regardless of the refund toggle — no additional action is required.
