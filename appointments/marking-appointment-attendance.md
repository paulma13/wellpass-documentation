# How to mark appointment attendance

## Overview

After a client attends their appointment, you can update the attendance status to reflect what happened. The three statuses available are **Confirmed**, **Attended**, and **No-show**. Statuses are updated from the appointment detail modal.

---

## How to mark attendance

1. Go to **Calendar** in the left sidebar and switch to the **Appointments** view.
2. Find the appointment card on the board and click it to open the **appointment detail modal**.
3. Locate the attendance status control in the modal.
4. Select the appropriate status:
   - **Confirmed** — The appointment is booked and the client is expected. This is the default state before the session.
   - **Attended** — The client showed up and completed the appointment.
   - **No-show** — The client did not show up for their appointment.

The status is saved immediately.

---

## Attendance statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | The appointment is active and the client is expected. |
| **Attended** | The client attended the session. |
| **No-show** | The client did not appear for their scheduled appointment. |
