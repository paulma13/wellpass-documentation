# How to assign appointment services to staff

## Overview

Use this when you need to set up which services a staff member offers so they can be booked for appointments. If a coach is not showing up as an option when you try to book a specific service, it is usually because that service has not been linked to their profile yet. You do this from the staff member's profile on the Staff page, under the Services tab. You will also need to make sure the Appointments role is enabled on their profile — without it, the staff member will not appear on the appointments board at all, even if they have services assigned. Studio owners typically do this when onboarding a new staff member or when adding a new service type to an existing coach's offering.
---

## How to assign a service to a staff member

1. Go to **Staff** in the left sidebar.
2. Find the staff member and open their profile.
3. Click the **Services** tab.
4. Select the services this staff member offers.
5. Save the changes.

The staff member can now be selected when booking appointments for those services.

---

## The Appointments role toggle

In addition to having services assigned, a staff member must have the **Appointments** role enabled to appear on the appointments board.

1. Open the staff member's profile.
2. Go to the **Profile** tab.
3. In the **Role** section, check the **Appointments** checkbox.
4. Save the changes.

When this toggle is on, the staff member's column appears on the appointments board. When it is off, they are not shown on the board even if they have services assigned.

---

## Why services are assigned per staff member

Not every staff member offers every service. Assigning services individually means that when a client or staff member selects a service to book, only the staff who actually offer it are available as options. This prevents incorrect bookings and keeps the board clean.
