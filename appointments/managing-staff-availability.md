# How to manage staff availability for appointments

## Overview

Use this when you need to set up or update when a staff member is available for appointments — their regular working hours, days off, or one-off blocks of unavailable time. Getting this right is what makes the appointments board useful: slots outside a staff member's schedule show as dark grey and cannot be booked, and blocked times show as striped so you and your team can see availability at a glance. You can set a recurring weekly schedule for each person individually, add a one-off block for a specific time, or use bulk scheduling to set hours for multiple staff members at once. Studio owners typically do this when onboarding new staff, at the start of a new term, or whenever a coach's hours change.

---

## Setting a staff member's availability schedule

1. Go to **Staff** in the left sidebar.
2. Find the staff member and open their profile.
3. Click the **Appointments Availability** tab.
4. Click the **Manage availability** button. A calendar modal opens.
5. Set the time ranges when this staff member is available to take appointments.
6. Save the schedule.

The staff member's working hours are reflected on the appointments board — time outside their schedule appears as dark grey (outside schedule).

---

## Adding an availability block

An availability block marks a specific time range as unavailable for a staff member, even if it falls within their regular schedule. Use this for one-off situations such as a personal commitment, a training day, or time off.

1. Open the staff member's profile and go to the **Appointments Availability** tab.
2. Click **Manage availability** to open the calendar modal.
3. Add a block by selecting the time range you want to mark as unavailable.
4. Save the block.

Blocked times appear as grey striped slots on the appointments board and cannot be booked.

> **Note:** Availability blocks are defined by a time range only. There is no reason or note field.

---

## Bulk scheduling

The **Schedule Staff** button on the **Staff** page lets you assign availability to multiple staff members at once. Use this when setting up schedules for a new week or when multiple staff have the same working hours.

1. Go to **Staff** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **Schedule Staff** button.
3. Select the staff members and set the schedule details.
4. Save to apply the schedule across all selected staff.

---

## How availability appears on the board

Each state is visually distinct on the appointments board:

| State | Appearance |
|---|---|
| Available | White / empty slot |
| Blocked | Grey striped |
| Outside schedule | Dark grey |
| Booked | Magenta appointment card |
