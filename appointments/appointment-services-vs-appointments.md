# Appointment services and appointments — what's the difference

## What an appointment service is

An **appointment service** is a template — the blueprint for a type of one-on-one session you offer at your studio. It holds all the default information: the name, type, description, cover image, duration, price, gender restriction, minimum advance booking time, and cancellation window.

Think of it like a recipe. A service called "60-Minute Private Reformer" defines what the session is, how long it lasts, and what it normally costs. The service itself has no date or time — it does not appear on your calendar. It just sits in your service library, ready to be booked.

Appointment services are managed from the **Appointments** page.

## What an appointment is

An **appointment** is a specific booking of a service for a particular client with a particular staff member at a particular date and time.

Using the recipe analogy: if the service is the recipe, an appointment is the actual session you schedule for a specific client on a specific afternoon. The same service — "60-Minute Private Reformer" — can be booked many times a day by different clients with different coaches.

Appointments appear on the **Appointments board** on the Calendar page. Each appointment can override the service's default price if needed.

Key things you can do at the appointment level:
- Assign a specific staff member and time slot
- Apply a client's package or record a cash booking
- Add notes for the session
- Update, reschedule, or cancel the booking
- Mark the client as attended or no-show after the session

## Why it works this way

Separating the service from the individual booking gives you flexibility without repetition.

You define each service once — its name, what type it is, duration, and default settings — and then book it as many times as needed for different clients. Want to offer private Pilates reformer sessions, stretch therapy, and nutritional consultations? Create three services, then book them on demand from the calendar.

It also means each booking can be personalised without changing the service template. You can adjust the price for a specific client or add session notes, without affecting the service definition or any other appointments.
