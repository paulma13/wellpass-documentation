# Assigning a package to a client

## Overview

Use this when a client has purchased or earned a package and you need to add it to their account manually. Packages are assigned from the client's profile and take effect immediately — sessions become available as soon as the package is assigned.

## How to assign a package

1. Go to the **Clients** page in the left sidebar.
2. Find the client and click their row to open their profile.
3. Click the **Packages** tab.
4. Click **+ Assign Package**.
5. The assign package modal opens and shows all active package templates for your studio.
6. Click the package you want to assign. Each package card shows the package name, the number of sessions, the validity period (if set), the standard price, and whether it applies to classes, appointments, or both.
7. After selecting a package, you are taken to the payment details step.
8. Set the **Payment method**. Options are:
   - **Cash**
   - **Online Card**
   - **Free / Complimentary**
9. Set the **Price paid**. This defaults to the package's standard price. You can change it to any amount. If you select **Free / Complimentary** as the payment method, the price is automatically set to zero and cannot be changed.
10. Click **Confirm** to assign the package.

The package appears immediately in the client's Packages tab with an **Active** status and a full session count.

## Settings and options

### Payment method

The payment method you select is recorded with the assignment and appears in your reports. Choose the method that reflects how the client actually paid.

- **Cash** — for in-person cash payments.
- **Online Card** — for card payments processed outside the app.
- **Free / Complimentary** — sets the price to zero. Use this for gifted or promotional packages. A complimentary package is recorded with a price of zero in your revenue reports.

### Price paid

The price paid defaults to the package's standard price but can be overridden. The amount you enter is what is recorded in your revenue reports — it does not affect the number of sessions in the package.

### Going back

If you selected the wrong package, click **Back** on the payment details step to return to the package list and choose a different one.

## Edge cases

- If your studio has no active package templates, the modal shows "No active packages found for this studio." You need to create a package template in your settings before you can assign one.
- Once assigned, the package is immediately active. There is no draft or pending state.
