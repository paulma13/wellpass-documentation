# How walk-in clients link to Wellpass

## Overview

A client who starts as a walk-in can later become linked to a Wellpass account. When this happens, all their past bookings and history at your studio are retroactively connected to their account. There are two ways this can happen: automatically, or when you update their phone number.

## Automatic linking

When a walk-in client creates a Wellpass account and provides the same phone number you have on file for them, the system automatically links their records across all studios. You do not need to do anything.

After automatic linking:

- The **Wellpass Account** chip appears on their profile.
- The **Edit** button disappears from their profile header, because their account is now managed through the Wellpass app.
- The client can see all their historical bookings — including past classes and appointments at your studio — in their Wellpass app.

**Prerequisite:** For automatic linking to work, the client must enter their phone number when they sign up for a Wellpass account. If they skip the phone number step during onboarding, linking does not happen — even if you have their phone number on file.

## Manual linking through a phone update

If you edit a walk-in client's phone number from their profile, and the new phone number matches an existing Wellpass account, the link is created as soon as you save the change.

After saving, a notice appears on screen confirming that the records were linked and showing the name on the Wellpass account. The client's profile is then updated to reflect their Wellpass status.

This can be useful if a client gave you an incorrect phone number when they first visited, or if their number has changed and the new one is tied to their Wellpass account.

## What does not change after linking

Linking does not affect past booking records, payments, or package history in your studio. Everything that was recorded before the link remains in place — it simply becomes visible to the client in their app.
