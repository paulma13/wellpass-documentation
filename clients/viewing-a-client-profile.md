# Viewing a client profile

## Overview

A client's profile gives you a full picture of their relationship with your studio: their visit stats, spending, active packages, and booking history. Open any client's profile from the Clients page by clicking their row in the table.

## The profile header

At the top of every profile you will see:

- **Avatar** — initials or a profile image.
- **Name** — the client's full name.
- **Wellpass Account chip** — shown only for clients linked to a Wellpass account. If this chip is visible, their name and email are managed through their Wellpass app, not your studio records.
- **Email** — shown only for clients linked to a Wellpass account, or for walk-in clients who have an email on file.
- **Phone** — the phone number on file.
- **Edit button** — shown only for walk-in clients (clients without a Wellpass account). Click it to update their name, phone, or email. This button is not shown for Wellpass-linked clients because their account is managed through the app.

## The Overview tab

The **Overview** tab is the default view when you open a profile. It shows:

**Spending summary**
- First Visit and Last Visit dates, shown as pills.
- Total spending callout — the combined revenue from packages, class bookings, and appointment bookings.
- A breakdown of that total split into three categories: Classes, Appointments, and Packages.

**Classes statistics**
- Booked, Attended, Cancelled, No-shows, and attendance percentage.

**Appointments statistics**
- Booked, Completed, Cancelled, and No-shows.

## The Packages tab

The **Packages** tab lists all packages ever assigned to this client, with the most relevant ones shown prominently. Each package entry shows:

- Package name and a status badge: **Active**, **Expired**, or **Exhausted**.
- A progress bar showing sessions remaining.
- The expiry date.
- The price paid.

From this tab you can:

- Click **+ Assign Package** to manually assign a new package to the client.
- Click the trash icon on an active package to remove it. Only active packages show the trash icon — expired and exhausted packages cannot be removed.

## The History tab

The **History** tab lists every class and appointment this client has had at your studio. If your studio offers both classes and appointments, a segmented toggle at the top lets you filter between the two. Each row shows:

- Class or service name.
- Date and time.
- Instructor or staff member.
- A status badge (for example: Attended, Cancelled, No-show).
- Payment method and price paid.
