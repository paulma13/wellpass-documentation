# Understanding the Clients page

## Overview

The **Clients** page gives you a paginated list of every client who has ever been booked into a class or appointment at your studio. Use it to find a client, review their visit history, manage their packages, or view their spending. The page shows 20 clients at a time.

## What the table shows

Each row in the table represents one client. The columns are:

- **Name** — the client's full name, shown with an avatar or initials.
- **Phone** — the phone number on file for the client.
- **Email** — the email address on file, if one exists.
- **Member Since** — the date of the client's first visit to your studio.
- **Last Visit** — the date of the client's most recent visit.
- **Packages** — shows how many packages the client currently has active (for example, "2 active"). If the client has no active packages, this column shows a dash.

## How to search for a client

Type a client's name into the search field in the top-right corner of the page. Results filter as you type. The search is slightly delayed to avoid filtering with every keystroke — results appear within a moment of you pausing.

## How to sort the table

Click any of the following column headers to sort the table by that column:

- **Name**
- **Last Visit**
- **Member Since**
- **Packages**

Click the same header again to reverse the sort order between ascending and descending.

## How clients appear on this page

Clients are added to this page automatically when they are booked into a class or appointment. There is no manual "Add client" button on the Clients page. The first time someone is booked — whether they are a Wellpass account holder or a new walk-in — they appear in the list.

If no bookings have been made yet, the page shows: "Clients will appear here once they book a class or appointment."

## Who can access this page

The Clients page is available to studio owners and admins. Receptionists do not have access to this page and are redirected if they try to open it.

## Opening a client's profile

Click any row in the table to open that client's profile. The profile shows their overview stats, package history, and booking history.
