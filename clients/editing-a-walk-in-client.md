# Editing a walk-in client

## Overview

You can update the name, phone number, or email address of any walk-in client directly from their profile. This is useful when a client gives you updated contact information, or when you need to correct a typo made when they were first added. This option is only available for walk-in clients — clients with a Wellpass account manage their own details through the app.

## How to edit a walk-in client

1. Go to the **Clients** page in the left sidebar.
2. Find the client and click their row to open their profile.
3. Click the **Edit** button in the profile header.
4. The edit form opens with their current details pre-filled.
5. Update any of the following fields:
   - **First name** (required)
   - **Last name** (required)
   - **Email** (optional)
   - **Phone** (required)
6. Click **Save** to apply the changes.

## What can be changed

You can update all four fields: first name, last name, phone number, and email. Email is optional — you can add it, change it, or leave it blank.

## Side effect: linking to a Wellpass account

If you save a phone number that matches an existing Wellpass account, the walk-in client's record is immediately linked to that account. A notice appears after saving that confirms the link and shows the name on the Wellpass account.

After linking, the client's profile is updated: the **Wellpass Account** chip appears in the header, and the **Edit** button is no longer shown. Their contact details are now managed through the Wellpass app.

## When the Edit button is not shown

The **Edit** button only appears for walk-in clients. If a client is already linked to a Wellpass account, the Edit button is not shown on their profile and their details cannot be changed from the staff-web app.
