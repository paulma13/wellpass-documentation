# Walk-in clients vs. Wellpass clients

## What it is

Every client in your studio is one of two types: a **walk-in client** or a **Wellpass client**. The difference is whether they have a Wellpass account linked to their record. A client's type determines what information is stored, what they can see, and whether they receive app notifications.

## Why it matters

Understanding the difference helps you know what to expect when you add or manage a client:

- Walk-in clients do not receive push notifications from the Wellpass app because they have no app account.
- Wellpass clients can see all their bookings — including past bookings at your studio — directly in their Wellpass app.
- Some profile actions (such as editing a name or phone number) are only available for walk-in clients, not for Wellpass clients.

## How each type works in Wellpass

### Walk-in clients

A walk-in client has no Wellpass account. Their identity is stored in your studio's records with a first name, last name, and phone number. Email is optional. You can edit their information at any time from their profile.

Walk-in clients can be booked into classes and appointments just like any other client. Their bookings and spending are tracked in your studio.

### Wellpass clients

A Wellpass client has a Wellpass app account. Their name and email are pulled from their Wellpass account — not stored separately in your studio records. A **Wellpass Account** chip appears on their profile to indicate the link. Their profile does not show an Edit button, because their account details are managed through the Wellpass app.

### Starting as a walk-in and becoming linked

A client can start as a walk-in and later become linked to a Wellpass account. This happens automatically when the client creates a Wellpass account using the same phone number you have on file for them. When linking occurs, all their past bookings at your studio become visible in their app. You do not need to do anything for this to happen.

It can also happen manually: if you update a walk-in client's phone number and it matches an existing Wellpass account, the link is created immediately when you save the change.
