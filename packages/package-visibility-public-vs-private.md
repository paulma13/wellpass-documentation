# Package visibility — public vs private

## What it is

Every package template has a **visibility setting**: Public or Private. This controls whether clients can see and purchase the package on their own through the Wellpass app, or whether it is only accessible when a staff member assigns it manually.

## Public packages

A public package is visible to all clients browsing the Wellpass app. Clients can view it, select it, and purchase it without any action from your staff. Use public packages for standard bundles you sell to everyone — for example, a 10-class bundle at your posted price.

When a package is public and active, it appears in the Wellpass app immediately. When you deactivate it or switch it to private, it is removed from the app.

## Private packages

A private package is invisible to clients in the Wellpass app. It can only be assigned by a staff member from the Packages page. Clients never see it, cannot search for it, and cannot purchase it themselves.

Use private packages for:

- Loyalty rewards or thank-you packages for long-term clients
- Complimentary or comp packages you give at no charge
- Staff or instructor discounts
- Promotional offers you want to manage case by case

## The free package rule

A free package cannot be set to public. If you toggle a package to free and also set it to public, the app blocks you from saving and shows a warning: "A free package cannot be public — anyone would be able to claim it without paying. Set a price or switch it to Private to continue."

Free packages must always be private. This ensures they can only be assigned manually by your staff to the clients you choose.

## Changing visibility

You can switch a package between public and private at any time by editing it. Switching to private removes it from the Wellpass app immediately — new clients can no longer find or purchase it. Switching to public makes it discoverable right away.

Changing the visibility of a package does not affect client packages that have already been assigned or purchased. Clients who already own a copy keep their sessions and expiry dates regardless of any visibility change.
