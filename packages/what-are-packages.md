# What are packages?

## What it is

A package is a bundle of sessions that a client can use to attend classes, appointments, or both. You define the bundle once as a **package template** — for example, "10-Class Bundle" — and then assign individual copies of that template to clients. Each copy a client owns is a **client package**.

## Why it matters

Packages let you sell session bundles upfront rather than charging per visit. They give clients a prepaid balance of sessions to draw from and give you predictable revenue. You can offer packages for classes, appointments, or both, and you can control whether clients can purchase them directly through the Wellpass app or whether your staff assigns them manually.

## How it works in Wellpass

**Package templates** live in the **Packages** tab on the Packages page. This is where you create and manage the bundles your studio offers — their names, session counts, prices, and which services they cover.

**Client packages** are the individual copies that clients own. When you assign a package to a client, or when a client purchases a public package through the Wellpass app, the system creates a client package linked to that template. Client packages appear in the **Client Packages** tab on the Packages page, and also on the individual client's profile.

**Session deduction** happens automatically. Each time a client attends a class or appointment that is covered by one of their packages, one session is deducted from that client package. The package tracks remaining sessions in real time.

**Status changes** happen based on usage and time. When a client's session count reaches zero, the status changes to **Exhausted**. When the package's validity period passes its expiry date, the status changes to **Expired** — even if sessions remain unused.
