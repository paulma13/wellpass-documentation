# Editing a staff member's profile

## Overview

Use this when you need to update a staff member's title, role, or pay rates — for example, if someone takes on a new role, starts providing a different service type, or has a pay rate change. You can also update the contact details (phone and email) for staff members who are still pending registration. All of this is done through the staff member's profile, which you open by clicking their name in the staff table.

## How to open a staff member's profile

1. On the **Staff** page, click the row of the staff member you want to edit.
2. A panel or modal opens showing their profile. The **Profile** tab is shown by default.

Note: clicking into a staff profile is not available to receptionists.

## What the Profile tab shows

The Profile tab displays the staff member's contact and role information:

- Email
- Phone
- Title
- Role (whether they are a Class Instructor, Appointment Provider, or both)
- Class pay rate (shown if they have the Class Instructor role)
- Appointment pay rate (shown if they have the Appointment Provider role)

## How to edit a staff member's details

1. On the **Profile** tab, click **Edit**.
2. Update any of the following fields:
   - **Title** — their job title as it will appear in the app and to clients
   - **Role** — use the checkboxes to enable or disable **Classes** and **Appointments** for this staff member
   - **Class pay rate** — shown when the Classes checkbox is on
   - **Appointment pay rate** — shown when the Appointments checkbox is on
3. Save your changes.

## Editing contact details for pending staff

For staff members who are still in **Pending** status — meaning they have not yet registered in the Wellpass management app — the **Profile** tab also allows you to edit their phone number and email directly. These fields are not editable for Active staff because their contact information is tied to their personal account.

To update a pending staff member's phone or email, open their profile by clicking **Edit** on their row in the staff table, then make the changes on the Profile tab.

## Edge cases

**Removing a role that has active assignments**: If a staff member is currently assigned to upcoming classes or appointments and you remove the relevant role, check those assignments after saving. The system does not automatically unassign them, but their capabilities for new assignments will reflect the updated role.

**Pay rate field disappearing**: Pay rate fields only appear when the corresponding role is enabled. If you turn off the Classes checkbox, the Class pay rate field will no longer be shown or saved. Turn the role back on if you need to set or view that rate.
