# Scheduling staff availability in bulk

## Overview

Use this when you want to schedule staff, set availability, set working hours, or set up when a staff member is available for appointments. This is the primary and recommended way to configure availability in Wellpass — whether you are setting up one person's hours or applying the same schedule to your entire appointment team at once. You will find this under **Schedule Staff** on the Staff page. If you only need to make a specific, one-off adjustment — like blocking a single day or adding an extra slot — use the Availability Calendar on the staff member's individual profile instead.

## Before you start

**Schedule Staff** only works with staff members who meet both of these conditions:
- Their status is **Active**
- They have the **Appointment Provider** role enabled

The **Schedule Staff** button only appears on the Staff page when your studio has appointments enabled and at least one active appointment staff member exists. The button is not visible to receptionists.

## How to schedule staff in bulk

### Step 1: Select staff members

1. On the **Staff** page, click **Schedule Staff**.
2. A list of eligible staff members appears as selectable chips.
3. Click the chips for the people you want to include. Selected chips are highlighted.
4. Use **Select all** to include everyone, or **Clear** to start over.
5. When you have the right people selected, proceed to the next step.

### Step 2: Configure the schedule

Fill in the following fields:

**Branch** (required)
Select which branch this schedule applies to from the dropdown.

**Date range**
Choose the period you want to set availability for. Quick presets are available:
- This week
- Next week
- This month
- Next month
- 3 months

To set a custom period, use the **From** and **To** date pickers instead.

**Time slots**
Define the days and hours for the schedule:

1. Check the boxes for the days of the week you want to include (Mon through Sun).
2. Use the shortcut buttons to select a common group of days quickly: **Weekdays** (Mon–Fri), **Weekend** (Sat–Sun), or **Every day** (Mon–Sun).
3. Set the **Start time** and **End time** for those days.

**Adding multiple time slots**
If your staff members have different hours on different days — for example, 9 AM–1 PM on weekdays and 10 AM–2 PM on Saturdays — you can add a second (or third) time slot. Each slot has its own day selection and start/end times. Use the option to add another slot and configure it separately.

### Step 3: Save

Click **Save**. The button shows the number of staff members the schedule will be applied to — for example, "Save for 4 Staff Members". Confirm the save.

The availability is set for all selected staff members at once, for the branch and date range you configured.

## Edge cases

**Staff member not appearing in the selection list**: If someone is missing, check that their status is Active and that the Appointment Provider role is enabled on their profile. Pending or inactive staff and staff without the appointment provider role are not shown in the Schedule Staff flow.

**Schedule Staff button not visible**: The button only appears when your studio has appointments enabled and at least one active appointment provider exists. If neither condition is met, the button will not show.

**Overlapping existing availability**: Saving a bulk schedule for a date range that already has availability configured for some staff members may add to or overwrite existing slots. Review individual staff availability after a bulk save if precision is important.
