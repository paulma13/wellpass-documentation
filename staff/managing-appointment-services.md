# Managing appointment services for a staff member

## Overview

Use this when you need to control which types of appointments a staff member can be booked for — for example, adding a new service they are qualified to offer, or removing one they no longer provide. Services are the specific appointment types your studio offers (such as a 60-minute massage, a private pilates session, or a nutrition consultation), and each staff member needs to be assigned the services they personally provide. This is managed from the **Services** tab in a staff member's profile.

## How to open the Services tab

1. On the **Staff** page, click the row of the staff member you want to manage.
2. In their profile, click the **Services** tab.

The **Services** tab is only shown for staff members who have the Appointment Provider role enabled. If you do not see this tab, the staff member is not set up as an appointment provider. You can enable that role from the **Profile** tab.

## What the Services tab shows

The Services tab has two sections:

- **Assigned services** — the appointment types this staff member is currently set up to provide. Each one has a remove button (trash icon).
- **Available services** — appointment types your studio offers that have not yet been assigned to this staff member. These are the services you can add.

## How to add one or more services

1. In the available services section, check the box next to each service you want to assign. You can select as many as you need at once.
2. To assign every available service at once, click **Select all**.
3. To clear your selections, click **Deselect all**.
4. Confirm or save the selection.

The selected services move to the assigned section and the staff member can now be booked for them.

## How to remove a service

1. In the assigned services section, find the service you want to remove.
2. Click the trash icon next to it.

The service is removed from this staff member's assignments. Clients can no longer book that appointment type with this person. This does not delete the service from your studio — it only removes it from this staff member.

## Edge cases

**A service is not appearing in the available list**: If a service you expect to see is missing from the available list, it may already be assigned to this staff member (check the assigned section) or it may not yet be created in your studio's service settings. Services are managed separately in your settings.

**Removing a service with upcoming bookings**: Removing a service does not automatically cancel existing appointments for that service. Clients who already have bookings with this staff member for the removed service will keep those appointments. Review upcoming appointments if needed after removing a service.
