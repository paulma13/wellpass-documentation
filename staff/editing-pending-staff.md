# Editing a pending staff member's details

## Overview

Use this when you realize you entered incorrect information for a staff member you recently added — most commonly a wrong phone number. As long as their status is still **Pending**, you can correct their details at any point. This matters most for the phone number, because that is what Wellpass uses to match the staff member to your studio. You may need to do this before the staff member has registered, or after — for example, if they already downloaded the app and signed up with their real number but found no studio connection waiting for them. In both situations, correcting the phone number in the Edit modal is all that is needed.

## How to edit a pending staff member

1. On the **Staff** page, find the staff member with a **Pending** badge.
2. Click **Edit** in the actions column of their row.
3. The edit modal opens with the following editable fields:
   - **Phone number**
   - **First name**
   - **Last name**
   - **Email**
4. Make your corrections and save.

## Why the phone number matters

When a pending staff member downloads the Wellpass management app and registers, Wellpass matches their new account to your studio using the phone number you entered. If the number on file does not match the one they use when signing up, the automatic connection will not happen and they will remain Pending.

There are two situations where correcting the phone number resolves this:

**Staff member has not registered yet**: If you correct the phone number before the staff member signs up, the pending record now holds the right number. When they register with that number, the connection happens automatically.

**Staff member has already registered**: If the staff member already downloaded the app and created their account — but found no studio linked to their profile — it is likely because the number on file does not match what they used when registering. Correct the phone number in the Edit modal and Wellpass re-runs the lookup immediately. If the staff member's account is found, the connection completes right away and their status changes to Active. You do not need to contact the staff member or ask them to do anything further.

## What you cannot edit here

The Edit option for pending staff only covers contact details. Capabilities (Class Instructor, Appointment Provider) and pay rates are not editable from this modal. Those settings are available once the staff member is Active and you open their full profile.

## Edge cases

**Staff member registered successfully and is now Active**: Once a pending staff member registers and their status changes to Active, the Edit option on their pending row is no longer available because the pending record no longer exists. To update a staff member's profile information after they are Active, open their profile by clicking their row and use the Edit button on the **Profile** tab — though note that phone and email are no longer editable there, as those belong to their personal account.

**Staff member registered but is still showing as Pending**: If the staff member downloaded the app, created their account, and their status is still Pending, the phone number on file is likely wrong. The system looked for a match when they registered and did not find one. Use the Edit option on their pending row to correct the phone number. As soon as you save the updated number, Wellpass looks up their account and completes the connection — no further action needed from you or the staff member.

**Wrong name entered**: If you entered the wrong first or last name, you can correct that here as well. The name is used for display purposes in your staff list and does not affect the matching process at registration.
