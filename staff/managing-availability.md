# Managing appointment availability for a staff member

## Overview

Use this when you need to make a specific, one-off adjustment to a staff member's availability — for example, blocking a single day they are out, adding one extra time slot for a specific date, or removing a block to restore time. This is not the right place to set up regular working hours or a full availability schedule. If you want to set working hours, schedule a staff member, or set up their availability from scratch, use **Schedule Staff** on the Staff page instead. This calendar is for targeted, individual changes on top of an existing schedule.

## How to open the Appointments Availability tab

1. On the **Staff** page, click the row of the staff member you want to manage.
2. In their profile, click the **Appointments Availability** tab.

The **Appointments Availability** tab is only shown for staff members who have the Appointment Provider role enabled. If you do not see this tab, the staff member is not set up as an appointment provider. You can enable that role from the **Profile** tab.

## The availability summary view

The Appointments Availability tab shows a compact summary of the next 8 weeks. Each week appears as a row with a 7-day grid. Days when the staff member has availability show a dot and the time range they are available. Days with no availability show as empty.

This view is for reference only. To make changes, click **Manage availability** to open the full Availability Calendar.

## The Availability Calendar

The **Availability Calendar** is a full-screen weekly calendar view. You can move between weeks using the **Previous**, **Next**, and **Today** buttons.

A legend at the top explains the two visual states:
- **Available** — shown in a solid color. Clients can book during these times.
- **Not available** — shown with a striped gray pattern. Clients cannot book during these times.

## How to add availability

1. In the Availability Calendar, find the week and day you want to add availability for.
2. Click and drag on an empty (not yet available) area to draw a time block.
3. A confirmation modal appears asking you to select a **branch**.
4. Select the branch and confirm.

The time block is added to the staff member's schedule. Clients will be able to book appointments with this person during those hours.

## How to block time

Use this when a staff member is normally available but needs to be unavailable for a specific period — for example, a day off or a period they are out of the studio.

1. In the Availability Calendar, find the existing available time block you want to block.
2. Click and drag across that available block.
3. A confirmation modal appears. It will say that clients will not be able to book appointments during this time.
4. Confirm the block.

The selected time shows as striped gray. Clients can no longer book new appointments during that period. Existing appointments already booked in that window are not affected.

## How to remove a block

Use this to restore time that was previously blocked — for example, if a staff member is available again after an absence.

1. In the Availability Calendar, click on a striped gray (blocked) time slot.
2. A confirmation modal appears. It will say that removing the block will restore availability and clients will be able to book again.
3. Confirm.

The block is removed and the time returns to available. Clients can book appointments in that slot again.

## Edge cases

**Overlapping time blocks**: If you try to add availability that overlaps with an existing block, the confirmation step will make the outcome visible before you commit.

**Changes affect new bookings only**: Blocking or removing availability does not cancel appointments that are already booked. If you need to cancel existing appointments, do that separately from the appointments management area.
