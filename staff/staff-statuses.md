# Staff statuses

## What it is

Every staff member in your studio has a status shown as a colored badge next to their name in the staff table. The status tells you whether that person is connected and available, inactive, or still waiting to complete their registration.

## Why it matters

The status determines what a staff member can do and how they appear across your studio. An Active staff member can be assigned to classes and appointments. A Pending staff member cannot be fully used yet because they have not registered in the app. Understanding what each status means helps you know whether you need to take action or simply wait.

## How statuses work in Wellpass

### Active

Shown as a green badge. The staff member has a Wellpass management app account and is connected to your studio. They can be assigned to classes, assigned as an appointment provider, and managed through their profile. This is the normal working state.

### Inactive

Shown as a gray badge. The staff member's row appears dimmed in the table. An inactive staff member is still connected to the studio but is not currently working. They do not appear as available for new class or appointment assignments.

### Pending

Shown as an orange badge. This status means you have added the staff member to your studio but they have not yet registered in the Wellpass management app using the phone number you entered. Once they download the app and sign up with that phone number, the connection completes automatically and their status changes to Active. You do not need to do anything — the transition happens on its own.

## What to do if a Pending staff member never activates

If a staff member stays Pending for longer than expected, the most likely cause is that the phone number on file does not match the number they used (or will use) when registering. You can correct the phone number by clicking **Edit** on their pending row in the staff table.

There are two situations this covers:

**The staff member has not yet signed up**: Correct the number so the pending record holds the right phone number. When the staff member registers with that number, the connection will complete automatically.

**The staff member already signed up but found no studio in the app**: This means they registered with their real phone number, but it did not match what you entered. Correcting the number in the Edit modal triggers an immediate lookup. If their account is found, the connection completes right away and their status changes to Active — no action needed from the staff member.

If the staff member has not yet downloaded the app, share the download link with them and ask them to register using the exact phone number shown in their pending record.
