# Staff page overview

## What the Staff page is

The Staff page is where you manage everyone connected to your studio — instructors, appointment providers, and any other staff members. From here you can see who is active, invite new people to join, control what each person can do, and set their pay rates. It is the starting point for any task related to your team.

## What you can see and do

The page shows a table listing all staff connected to your studio. Each row displays the staff member's name and photo (or initials if no photo is set), their title, their current status, their role, their email, and their phone number. Pay rates are also shown in the table, but only to owners and admins — receptionists do not see this column.

Above the table, two action buttons may appear depending on your studio's setup:

- **Connect Staff** — adds a new staff member to your studio. This button is hidden from receptionists.
- **Schedule Staff** — sets availability for multiple staff members at once. This button only appears when your studio has appointments enabled and at least one active appointment staff member exists. It is also hidden from receptionists.

A filter bar above the table lets you view all staff or filter by **Classes** or **Appointments**. This filter only appears when your studio has both classes and appointments enabled.

## Role-based access

What you can do on the Staff page depends on your role:

- **Owners and admins** can view all columns, click into staff profiles, connect new staff, remove staff, and schedule availability.
- **Receptionists** can see the staff list but cannot click into a staff member's profile, cannot connect or remove staff, cannot schedule availability, and cannot see pay rates.

## Staff statuses

Each staff member has one of three statuses — **Active**, **Inactive**, or **Pending** — shown as a colored badge next to their name. These statuses reflect whether the staff member has registered in the app and whether their connection to your studio is live. See the Staff statuses article for a full explanation.
