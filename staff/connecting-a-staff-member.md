# Connecting a staff member

## Overview

Use this when you want to add a new instructor or staff member to your studio — whether they already use Wellpass at another studio or are brand new to the platform. You will enter their phone number and Wellpass will either connect them immediately or create a pending record that activates automatically once they sign up. This is the only way to bring a new person onto your team in the staff-web app.

## How to connect a staff member

1. On the **Staff** page, click **Connect Staff**.
2. Enter the staff member's phone number and click **Next** (or the equivalent confirm button).
3. One of two things happens at this point — see the two paths below.

### Path A: the staff member already has a Wellpass management app account

If the phone number matches an existing account, Wellpass connects them to your studio immediately. They appear in your staff table with an **Active** status right away. No further steps are needed, and the staff member does not need to do anything.

### Path B: no account found for that phone number

If no account is found, you are asked to fill in their details:

- **First name** (required)
- **Last name** (required)
- **Email** (optional)
- **Title** (optional — e.g. "Pilates Instructor")

After you save, a **Pending** record is created for this staff member. Once they download the Wellpass management app and register using that same phone number, the connection to your studio happens automatically. Their status changes from Pending to Active with no action required from you.

## Setting capabilities and pay rates

During the Connect Staff flow, you also configure what the staff member can do at your studio:

**Capabilities** — shown based on what your studio has enabled:
- **Class Instructor** toggle — turn on if this person teaches classes
- **Appointment Provider** toggle — turn on if this person provides appointments

**Pay rates** — a pay rate field appears for each capability you enable:
- **Class pay rate** ($/session) — shown when Class Instructor is turned on
- **Appointment pay rate** ($/session) — shown when Appointment Provider is turned on

These settings can be changed later from the staff member's profile.

## Edge cases

**Phone number already connected**: If you enter a phone number that is already linked to a staff member at your studio, an error message is shown. You cannot connect the same person twice. Check your existing staff list to confirm they are already there.

**Wrong phone number entered**: If you create a Pending record but realize you typed the wrong number, you can correct it at any point while the staff member's status is still Pending. Open the **Edit** option on their pending row and update the phone number. If the staff member has not yet registered, the corrected number is what Wellpass will use to match them when they sign up. If the staff member has already registered with their real number but found no studio linked to their account, correcting the phone number triggers an immediate lookup — if their account is found, the connection completes right away and their status changes to Active.
