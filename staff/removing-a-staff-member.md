# Removing a staff member

## Overview

Use this when a staff member no longer works at your studio and you need to disconnect them from your account — for example, if someone has left the studio or was added by mistake. Removing a staff member ends their connection to your studio. This action is available to owners and admins only. Receptionists do not see the remove option.

## How to remove a staff member

1. On the **Staff** page, find the staff member you want to remove in the table.
2. Click **Remove** in the actions column of their row.
3. A confirmation modal appears with the message: "Are you sure you want to remove [Name] from your studio? They won't be connected to your studio anymore."
4. Click **Remove** (shown in red) to confirm, or **Cancel** to go back without making changes.

The staff member is removed from your studio and no longer appears in your staff list.

## What removing a staff member does

Removing a staff member ends their connection to your studio. They lose access to your studio's data and settings in the Wellpass management app. Their personal Wellpass account is not deleted — they can still use the app and connect to other studios if applicable.

## What removing a staff member does not do

Removing a staff member does not automatically cancel any upcoming classes or appointments they are assigned to. Before removing someone, review their upcoming schedule and reassign or cancel those sessions as needed.

## Edge cases

**Removing a pending staff member**: You can remove a staff member who is still in Pending status. Since they have not yet registered, removing them simply deletes the pending record. They will not be notified.

**Reconnecting a removed staff member**: If you remove a staff member by mistake or want to bring them back later, you can reconnect them using the **Connect Staff** flow. Their phone number will be used to find their account if they already have one.
