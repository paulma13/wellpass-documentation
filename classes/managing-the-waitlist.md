# Managing the waitlist

## Overview

When a class session reaches its maximum capacity, clients can join a waitlist through the Wellpass app. Staff can view everyone waiting — either per session or across all of today's sessions at once — and manually promote any waitlisted client to a confirmed booking when a spot becomes available. There is no automatic promotion; every promotion requires a deliberate staff action.

## How the waitlist works

Clients join the waitlist through the Wellpass client app when a session is full. The system assigns each client a numbered position in the order they joined.

When a staff member promotes a client from the waitlist, the system creates a confirmed booking for that client and marks their waitlist entry as promoted. The client receives a notification on their Wellpass app. Promotions are not automatic — when a booked client cancels their spot, the system does not move anyone off the waitlist on its own. Staff must review the waitlist and decide whether and whom to promote.

If an entire session is cancelled, all clients currently on the waitlist are notified. Walk-in clients added to the waitlist without a linked Wellpass account do not receive notifications.

## How to view the waitlist

There are two places to view waitlisted clients.

**Today's waitlist — dashboard**

The **Waitlist** card on the main dashboard shows every client waiting on any of today's sessions. Each row displays the client's name, their position number, the class name, and the session start time. Use this view to get a quick overview of who is waiting across all sessions today.

To promote a client from this view, click **Admit** next to their name. The system immediately creates a confirmed booking and removes them from the waiting list.

**Per-session waitlist — session detail panel**

To see the waitlist for a specific session, open the calendar and click the session card. If any clients are waiting, a **Waitlist** section appears at the bottom of the panel, below the confirmed bookings list. Each row shows the client's name and their position number.

To promote a client from this view:

1. Click **Accept** next to the client's name.
2. A confirmation prompt appears. If the session is already at full capacity, the prompt notes this and asks whether you want to promote the client anyway.
3. Click **Yes, Promote** to confirm.

The client moves from the waitlist to a confirmed booking and the panel refreshes.

**Adding a client to the waitlist manually**

If a session is full and a client is present at the studio without a waitlist entry, you can add them manually. Open the session detail panel and click **+ Add to Waitlist** in the header. This button appears automatically when the session is at capacity.

## Edge cases

**Promoting when the session is already full**

You can promote a waitlisted client even if the session has no free spots. The confirmation prompt will warn you that the session is full and show the current booking count. Confirming the promotion exceeds the session's capacity limit. Use this only when you intend to accept an over-capacity situation.

**Walk-in clients on the waitlist**

Walk-in clients added by staff who do not have a linked Wellpass account can be placed on the waitlist and promoted to a confirmed booking. However, they do not receive notifications at any point, including if the session is cancelled.

**Cancelling a session with clients on the waitlist**

When you cancel an entire class session, all waiting clients are notified automatically. Their waitlist entries are marked as cancelled. No further action is required for the waitlist when cancelling a session.
