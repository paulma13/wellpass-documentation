# How to cancel a client's booking

## Overview

Use this when one specific client needs to be removed from a class — because they called to cancel, something came up, or you need to free their spot for someone else. This removes, drops, or deletes that one client's booking without touching any other bookings on the session or cancelling the class itself. The client gets notified automatically through the app, their package credit is returned right away if they paid with a package, and if they paid by card online you can choose whether to issue a refund at the same time. Studio owners and front desk staff use this throughout the day whenever a client needs to be taken off a session.

## How to cancel a client's booking

1. Open the **Calendar** from the main navigation.
2. Click the class session card for the session that contains the booking you want to cancel. The session detail panel opens.
3. Scroll to the **Booked** list. Each confirmed booking shows the client's name, phone number, and a payment badge (Package, Card, or Cash).
4. Locate the client whose booking you want to cancel.
5. Click the red **X** icon on the right side of that client's row. A **Cancel Booking** confirmation dialog opens.
6. Review the confirmation. What happens next depends on how the client paid:
   - **Package** — the dialog confirms their session will be returned to their package automatically. No further action needed.
   - **Online card** — a toggle appears: **Refund online card payment**. Turn it on to issue a refund to the client's card. Leave it off to cancel without a refund.
   - **Cash** — only the client's name is shown. No refund option appears.
7. Click **Yes, Cancel** to confirm. Click **Keep** to go back without making changes.

After confirmation, the booking row updates to show a red **Cancelled** badge. The client's spot is released and the session capacity count decreases by one.

## Settings and options

**Refund toggle (online card bookings only)**

When cancelling a booking paid by online card, the **Refund online card payment** toggle controls whether a refund is sent to the client's card.

- Toggle on — the app processes the refund immediately. A confirmation message shows the refund amount on success.
- Toggle off — the booking is cancelled but no refund is issued.

If the refund cannot be processed, the booking is not cancelled. An error message tells you to contact the Wellpass team to process the refund manually.

**Package bookings**

If the client paid using a package, one session is automatically returned to their package the moment you confirm the cancellation. No further action is needed.

**Cash bookings**

Cash bookings have no automatic refund. Any cash refund to the client must be handled outside the app.

## Edge cases

**The refund fails**

If a card refund fails, the booking stays active and is not cancelled. The app shows an error message asking you to contact the Wellpass team. You can attempt the cancellation again at any time.

**The booking has already been cancelled**

Cancelled bookings appear in the client list with a red **Cancelled** badge and no action icons. You cannot cancel them again. If the refund was not processed at the time of cancellation, an orange **Refund not processed** badge also appears on that row.

**The client is a walk-in**

Walk-in clients added manually without a Wellpass account do not receive a push notification when their booking is cancelled. The booking is still cancelled and the session capacity is updated.

**The session has already started or ended**

You can still cancel an individual booking after the session has started or ended. The action icons remain visible for non-cancelled bookings regardless of session timing.
